# Appearance

Three things are chosen separately: the **wallpaper**, the **palette** (where
the colors come from) and the **mode** (light, dark or Auto). All three are
chosen in Settings → Appearance or in the strip (`Mod+Alt+A`).

## Wallpapers

The grid shows the `picker.wallpaperDir` folder (`~/Pictures/Wallpapers` by
default). Thumbnails are generated once with ffmpeg (400 px) in
`~/.cache/spore/thumbs`, all together in a single process, and regenerated
only if the original changes.

When you choose one, `shell.qml` copies it to
`~/.local/state/spore/wallpaper.jpg` (and saves the original's path in
`wallpaper-source`, to mark it in the grid). That copy is the one used by the
background, the Wallpaper palette, the lockscreen and the greeter. With
nothing chosen, `assets/wallpapers/spore-dome.jpg` is used.

The header of the Control Center's Home page doesn't load the whole
wallpaper: it uses a crop already at its size, `~/.cache/spore/cc-hero.jpg`,
made with ffmpeg when the wallpaper changes. Loading the original (a 4K PNG)
took about 150 ms every time the panel opened; the crop, 2 ms.

`assets/wallpapers/` ships the brand's five official ones: `spore-dome` (the
default), `spore-dome-night` (its dark version), `spore-gills`, `spore-cells`
and `spore-roots`. To see them in the grid, copy them to your wallpaper
folder.

### Transitions

`wallpaper.transition`: `none`, `fade`, `wipe`, `disc`, `nix` (hexagon
outlines that then shrink), `nix-rnd` (random hexagons), `spore` (the new one
grows from hundreds of spores that land and merge like a colony) or `random`.

The duration isn't configurable: each effect has the one that suits it best.
The simple ones take about a second (`fade` 0.9 s, `wipe` and `disc` 1 s),
and the hexagon and spore ones are longer so their phases can be seen (`nix`
and `nix-rnd` 1.5 s, `spore` 1.6 s).

They're our own shaders in `shell/wallpaper/shaders/`. The compiled
`.frag.qsb` files are committed, so neither the package nor dev mode needs
`qsb`; `build.sh` recompiles them after editing a `.frag`.

### Video wallpapers

`.mp4`, `.mov` and `.webm` files in the folder show up with a ▶. They play
once at login, on unlock and when chosen, and then stay on their last frame.
That frame is `wallpaper.jpg`: what stays as the background and what the
palette, the lockscreen and the greeter use.

- **A separate process:** the video is played by `video/shell.qml`, started
  by `wallpaper/VideoPlayer.qml`, which exits when done. The shell never
  loads QtMultimedia, and the decoder's ~330 MB (for a 4K video) go back to
  the system when it exits. Inside the shell they stayed held even after the
  `Video` was unloaded.
- **No transition:** the video already has its own. When you choose one, the
  background fades to black right away (so you can tell something started),
  and the process reports when there's a frame on screen.
- **The last frame, cached:** it's extracted along with the thumbnail, just
  once, in `~/.cache/spore/stills`. Choosing a video copies it from there
  instead of decoding it again.
- **Turned off** (Settings → Appearance → Video wallpapers, or
  `wallpaper.videos: false`): videos behave like images (their last frame)
  and nothing is started.
- **Which videos:** decoding is done in software. Short, light clips work
  much better than Apple's originals (10-bit HEVC at 240 fps): for example,
  10 s at 60 fps in 8-bit HEVC.
- nixpkgs' Quickshell doesn't include QtMultimedia: `bin/spore` adds the
  paths of `qt6.qtmultimedia` (the same Qt) and exports `SPORE_QUICKSHELL`
  for the video process.

## Palettes

`common/Theme.qml` defines each palette with its two variants (light and
dark) and the same roles: `base`, `crust`, `surface`, `text`, `accent`,
`textOnAccent`, `error`, the 16 terminal colors and Kate's. Every other color
of the design (`ink2`, `muted`, `cardBg`, `track`, `barBg`…) is derived from
those roles, so any palette works on every screen and in both modes.

| Palette | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| `spore` (default) | the brand: Mist, Ink and Spore in light mode; Soil, Bone and Peach in dark mode |
| `catppuccin` | Latte and Mocha |
| `ayu` | Light and Dark |
| `wallpaper` | [matugen](https://github.com/InioX/matugen) (Material 3) from the wallpaper |

Adding one means adding an entry to `palettes` and `paletteNames` in
`Theme.qml`; the greeter gets the same copy when its package is built.

The **Wallpaper** palette is generated by matugen in
`~/.local/state/spore/colors.json`, with both variants together. `shell.qml`
regenerates it at startup and when a wallpaper is chosen, only if the
wallpaper is newer than the JSON. It has no terminal colors (Material 3
doesn't define them and matugen's base16 comes out almost monochrome): it
uses Ayu's.

The **logo** doesn't change with the palette: it uses the brand's fixed
colors (`common/brand.js`), in its version for a light or dark background.

## Auto mode

Dark at night and light by day, from sunrise and sunset at the weather's
location (Settings → Lock screen → Location, or the time zone's city if it's
empty). `services/Sun.qml` computes it with no network (the NOAA algorithm,
~1 minute precision) and schedules the next change; it checks at least every
5 minutes, so it corrects itself after a suspend. The time zone's city and
its coordinates come from the system's zone table (tzdata's `zone1970.tab`),
never from the network, so Auto mode works offline and with the weather off.
Next to MODE, Settings shows until when the current mode lasts
("Light until 19:06").

## Other apps

`services/SystemTheme.qml` regenerates everything whenever the palette or
the mode changes:

| App | What it writes | When it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Portal / GTK3 | dconf: `color-scheme`, `gtk-theme`, `icon-theme` | live |
| GTK3 (Thunar, etc.) | its own theme, `~/.local/share/themes/spore-{a,b}`, which imports `adw-gtk3(-dark)` and `~/.config/gtk-3.0/thunar.css` and defines libadwaita's colors; `~/.config/gtk-3.0/gtk.css` stays empty | live: GTK3 only reads `gtk.css` at startup, but reloads the theme when `gtk-theme` changes, which is why it alternates between `a` and `b` |
| GTK4 / libadwaita | `~/.config/gtk-4.0/gtk.css` with the same colors (libadwaita ignores `gtk-theme`) | on reopen |
| kitty | `~/.local/state/spore/kitty-{dark,light}.conf`, linked as `~/.config/kitty/*-theme.auto.conf` (if `~/.config/kitty` exists) | live (SIGUSR1; on NixOS the process is called `.kitty-wrapped`) |
| Kate / KDE | the `[Colors:*]` groups of `~/.config/kdeglobals` and `Color Theme` in `katerc` | on reopen |
| niri | `~/.local/state/spore/niri.kdl` (border colors, focus-ring off), included at the end of niri's config with `include optional=true` | live |
| rofi | `~/.local/state/spore/rofi-colors.rasi`, which the rofi theme imports with `@import` (one that does: `examples/rofi/spore.rasi`) | the next time it opens |
| yazi | `~/.local/state/spore/yazi-theme.toml`, linked as `~/.config/yazi/theme.toml` | the next time it opens |
| Zed | `~/.local/state/spore/zed-theme.json` ("Spore Dark" and "Spore Light"), linked as `~/.config/zed/themes/spore.json`; in Zed: `theme.mode = "system"` and those two themes | the mode live; the palette when Zed reopens |

Nothing of yours is lost along the way. A `gtk.css` you wrote is kept as
`gtk.css.before-spore` before Spore's goes in its place; `kdeglobals` and
`katerc` are edited in place, and the first time a copy of each stays as
`.before-spore`. A link someone else manages (home-manager, your dotfiles)
is left alone, so is a real file where Spore would put a link, and that app
keeps its own theme.

## What the greeter sees

Whenever something the greeter shows changes (wallpaper, mode, palette,
`colors.json`), the shell publishes a copy in `/var/lib/spore/$USER/`. The
greeter needs no notification: it's a new process every time and reads the
copy at startup. See [greeter](greeter.md).
